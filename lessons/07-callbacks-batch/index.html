Callbacks & batch

Not everything needs a tween. ScrollTrigger can just tell you when things happen (callbacks), toggle a CSS class, or reveal many elements efficiently with ScrollTrigger.batch().

Try this (edit main.js)

  1. Scroll past the blue box and watch the log in the bottom-right corner.
  2. Change the batch stagger to 0.3 and interval to 0.5.
  3. Style .watch.is-active differently in index.html — class toggling means you can animate with plain CSS.
