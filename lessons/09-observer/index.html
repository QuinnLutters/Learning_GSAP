Observer + ScrollTo

Observer listens to wheel / touch / pointer gestures without scrolling anything. ScrollToPlugin animates the scroll position. Together: one flick = exactly one section.

Try this (edit main.js)

  1. Flick your trackpad once. Then change tolerance to 50 and compare.
  2. Change the scroll duration and ease inside goTo().
  3. Remove wheelSpeed: -1 — which direction is "up" now?
