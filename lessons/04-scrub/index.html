Scrub

With scrub, the animation stops having its own duration. Its progress is tied to scroll: 0% at start, 100% at end. Scroll back and it rewinds. The green bar at the top is a scrub over the whole page.

Try this (edit main.js)

  1. Scroll the lanes section quickly and stop suddenly. See how scrub: 3 "catches up" slowly.
  2. Give the scrub: true tween an ease: "bounce.out". (Scrubbed tweens default to ease: "none" — which is usually what you want.)
  3. Change the lanes' end to "+=300". Shorter scroll distance = faster animation.
