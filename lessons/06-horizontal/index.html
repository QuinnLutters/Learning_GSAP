Horizontal scroll

You scroll down, the content moves sideways. There's no magic here: it's a pinned section plus a scrubbed tween that moves a wide "track" to the left. Everything from lessons 04 and 05 combined.

Try this (edit main.js)

  1. Add a fifth .panel in index.html. The math adjusts on its own.
  2. Add snap: 1 / (panels.length - 1) to make it settle on whole panels.
  3. Look at the yellow square in panel 4: it uses containerAnimation to trigger based on horizontal position.
