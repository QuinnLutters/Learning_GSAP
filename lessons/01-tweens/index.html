Tweens

A tween animates properties of an element from one value to another over time. Every scroll animation you'll build later is just a tween (or timeline) with a scrollTrigger attached, so this is the foundation.

Try this (edit main.js)

  1. Change the ease in the to demo to "bounce.out", then "elastic.out(1, 0.3)".
  2. In the stagger demo, change each: 0.1 to 0.4, then try from: "center".
  3. Add backgroundColor: "#ff6b9a" to any tween — GSAP animates colors too.
